LTC4359 Channel Revision

Replace the LM74700 with an LTC4359 while keeping the Q3/Q1 back-to-back common-source arrangement. When SHDN is pulled low, GATE is pulled to SOURCE, both MOSFETs turn off together, and the output is truly disconnected. CH2 is the same circuit; see the cross-reference table in section 4 for its designators.

6Still to confirm before the board revision

  • Temperature

    LTC4359CMS8 is rated 0–70°C. If it will be used below 0°C or in a hot enclosure, use LTC4359IMS8 (−40–85°C) instead.

  • Turn-on

    Driving two IPT015N10N5 with 10µA takes about 30ms. If a heavy load is already present at turn-on, the MOSFETs will dwell in the linear region and the SOA must be checked; alternatively, use a 3–5mΩ 100V MOSFET with lower gate charge.

  • TVS

    Place D1/D2 as close to the IN pin of U1 as possible with short traces, so that a surge does not reach 100V before it is clamped.

  • SHDN

    Route SHDN away from high dv/dt traces such as SPI and switching regulators; place C01/C18 next to U1.

  • Firmware

    LMx_EN polarity is inverted: after the revision high = on (current board: high = off). The firmware will add a polarity macro in board.h; just switch it when changing boards.
