# X9-ATX-500 specification summary

Sources (read 2026-10-19):

- Product page: https://pico-box.com/product/x9-atx-500/
- User manual: https://pico-box.com/docs/x9-atx-500-user-manual/

## Input

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Voltage range | 16–60 V; output capability is optimized for 48 V and decreases away from 48 V |
| Input overvoltage | 60.2–60.5 V |
| Connector | Supplied 400 mm cable: one end is a 7.4 × 5.0 mm DC female jack (can be fixed to the chassis with two M3 screws), the power supply end is 6P C6P (same spec as Molex 39-01-2060) |
| Pinout | pin 1–3 positive, pin 4–6 negative |
| Not specified | Wire gauge, reverse polarity protection, inrush current, soft start, standby consumption |

## Output

| Voltage | Max | Peak | Accuracy |
|---|---|---|---|
| +12 V | 40 A | 45 A | ±1.5% |
| +5 V | 6 A | 8 A | ±1.5% |
| +5 VSB | 2.5 A | 3 A | ±1.5% |
| +3.3 V | 6 A | 8 A | ±1.5% |
| −12 V | 0.05 A | 0.1 A | ±5% |

- Total must not exceed 500 W; peaks must not exceed 60 seconds.
- Efficiency > 96% (no efficiency curve given).

## Control and timing

- PS_ON low turns the outputs on; high or open turns them off.
- Timing per Intel ATX 2.01: T1 power-on < 500 ms; T2 rise 0.1–20 ms; T3 PG delay 100–500 ms; T4 PG rise ≤ 10 ms; T5 hold-up ≥ 16 ms; T6 power-down warning ≥ 1 ms.

## Protection

- OCP, OVP, OTP, short circuit, no load (may latch up at no load).
- Overload at 110–160% shuts down and latches.
- OCP: +12 V 40–45 A; +5 V 8–10 A; +3.3 V 8–10 A.
- OVP (min / typ / max): +12 V 13.4 / 15 / 15.6 V; +5 V 5.74 / 6.3 / 7 V; +3.3 V 3.76 / 4.2 / 4.3 V.
- Not specified: OTP threshold, UVP, reverse polarity protection.

## Interfaces

| Connector | Use |
|---|---|
| J1 | 9P USB-to-UART status output: 115200, 8N1 |
| J2 | 4P multi-unit sync (NPSON, GND) |
| J3 | 4P PWM fan |
| P5 | Input (when units are paralleled, connect to the same 16–60 V supply) |

J1 packet: header `0xAA 0x55`, sequence number, status (00 IDLE, 02 RUN, 03 OVP), input current IN_A (mA), input voltage PGI (mV, already scaled), VS12_1, VS12_2, VS5, VS3 (mV), checksum. The manual does not give the field byte lengths or the checksum algorithm; these need to be measured.

## Mechanical and environmental

- 125 × 63 × 31 mm (SFX size); the base plate is compatible with standard SFX power supply mounting holes; hole dimensions are only on the manual drawings.
- Comes with a CNC heatsink (top) and base plate (bottom); weight 395 g (without cables), 592 g (with cables).
- Forced air cooling required at full load; 12 V must be derated until the temperature drops below 65 °C.
- Operating temperature −10 to 70 °C, linear derating above 40 °C.
- MTBF 100,000 hours (25 °C full load, MIL-HDBK-217F).
